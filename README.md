# Detroit Automaker Capital Allocation: GM vs. Ford vs. Stellantis (FY2016–FY2025)

Ten-year capital allocation study of GM, Ford and Stellantis, built from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings (62 annual 10-K/20-F filings, ~160K tagged facts).

**Key finding:** GM led with a 13.9% capital-weighted ROIC vs. 11.0% for Stellantis and −2.6% for Ford, yet its returns declined in every year of the decade. Captive finance arms (GM Financial, Ford Credit; 84–91% of parent debt) were separated to measure true automotive returns.
